About Us

RGK Group is diversified group with activities in agriculture, textile production (Looms), textile retail and agro processing unit. The group has flourished under the great guidance of Mr. Rameshji Kasat into all the above verticals of the industry. RGK Group has a very high brand reputation in north Karnataka for the best business practices and the quality products.

With great experience in handling the Textile Retail Business for more than 4 decades RGK Group has ventured into first agro processing unit "R G Kasat Industries Pvt. Ltd". Which is highly automated dry corn processing agro unit built at agro park developed by Central Government at Bagalkot.

Key Features of The Project

Bagalkot is One out of 5 Declared FOOD-AGRO Park from Central Government.

Bagalkot District was carved out of Bijapur in Year 1997. Bagalkot is divided into two parts-the new Bagalkot or Navanagar and the old Bagalkot town. The construction of new town became necessary following submergence of parts of old town due to construction of Almatti dam. Navanagar is a planned town with grid pattern layout with wide roads, parks, and other amenities.

Bagalkot produces approx. 20% of Karnataka state produced maize. With high availability of good crop every year and most months of the year, R G Kasat industries is in position to support with consistent quality of the Corn grits around the year to esteemed customers.

Our Plant has been set up with best agro processing machines supplied by Buhler (Switzerland), IMAS (turkey).

Our Products

Flaking grits

Corn Grits Product Image

Corn Grits (Coarse and Fine)

Based on the required corn grit particulate size we use our automated roller mills and plan sifter to support customer needs. These are used primarily in extruded snack food industry, breakfast cereals, breweries, confectionery and baking applications etc.

Corn Meal Product Image

Corn Meal

Finest quality of corn grits without flour is made to support the application of specialty snacks. The grits are below 1 mm size to support the extrusion with great efficiency.

Corn Flour

Manufacturing Process

Corn Receiving

Every truck received at our plant is analyzed for relevant parameters to get the best quality raw material for our production. Our processing area is well separated from grain stock area to keep complete hygiene, dust free processing area. Corn is sent by automated elevators to the first bins in Plant.

Corn Receiving Equipment Image

Cleaning

Corn is cleaned using the completely controlled cleaning equipment's like drum sieves, de-stoners, vibro-separators, Magnets etc. All our machines are supplied with pressurized air to remove the dust, fine impurities particles from the grain.

Cleaning Equipment Image

Conditioning

Corn is next fed into an automated moisture control unit. Here addition of water is done, to ensure that a constant level of moisture is attained.

Conditioning Unit Image

De-germination

Dampened Maize is added to our De Germinator which is supplied by BUHLER. BUHLER, world's best known manufacturer of agro processing machinery has made this patented MHXM-M De germinator. With the help of buhler de germinator by gentle treatment on the maize kernel, the germ, fiber and endosperm are neatly separated with minimal quantities of fines. Hence as germ separation is very efficiently done we get De-germinated kernels with low fat content. Corn flow rate to de germinator are monitored online to help us produce consistent quality product.

De-germinator Machine Image

Aspiration

Fines and husks generated by the de-germinator are removed by sifters and aspiration channels. Which are collected in closed area separated from main processing area.

Aspiration System Image

Plansifters

The mixture of de-germed maize and loosened maize germs are pneumatically lifted and graded by size on plan sifters.

Plansifter Machine Image

Purifiers

The finished products are passed over control sifters to ensure the removal of oversize or fine materials, prior to the transfer to the finished product bins.

Purifier Equipment Image

Control Sifting

The finished products are passed over control sifters to ensure the removal of oversize or fine materials, prior to the transfer to the finished product bins.

Control Sifting Equipment Image

Finished Products

Products separated by plan sifter, purifier are collected in different bins passing through magnets. This material is packed in our standard pack sizes or customer requested kindly of packages.
